# Product

## Register

brand

## Users

Small businesses and early-stage startups that need software built — websites, web apps, data workflows, workflow automation, AI-assisted operations — but don't have an in-house engineering team. They arrive evaluating whether this studio is capable and trustworthy enough to hand real work to. Their context is a decision-making one: comparing options, looking for evidence they can rely on before reaching out.

## Product Purpose

Loopers Lab is a small digital studio — two developers and one PM — that designs, builds, hosts, automates, and maintains websites, web apps, data workflows, and AI-assisted operations. The site's job is to build credibility and demonstrate capability: show what the team can do, how they work, and that they stick around to keep software running long after launch. Success is a qualified visitor who trusts the studio's competence and starts a conversation.

## Brand Personality

Handcrafted, approachable, trustworthy. The voice is an honest expert who explains rather than posturing — story-driven (the "two devs, one PM" origin story is an asset, not something to hide behind corporate anonymity). Warm and human, but competent and precise. It should feel like a real small team that cares about the work, not a faceless vendor.

## Anti-references

Mass-produced SaaS marketing templates: the hero-metric block (big number + label + gradient accent), endlessly repeated icon + heading + text card grids, and the tiny uppercase tracked eyebrow above every section. If a visitor could look at a page and say "AI/template made this," it has failed. Avoid flashy-but-empty agency effects (decorative glow, gradient text, glassmorphism used by reflex) that signal noise over substance.

## Design Principles

- **Show the humans behind it.** The team's craft and story are the differentiator. Make the real "two devs, one PM" studio visible instead of hiding behind stock corporate anonymity.
- **Earn trust through evidence.** Credibility comes from real work, real process, and real outcomes — not adjectives. Show, don't tell.
- **Built to last, and it shows.** The studio maintains software long after launch; the site itself should feel durable, cared-for, and considered, mirroring that promise.
- **Handcrafted over templated.** Every section should read as deliberately made, not assembled from stock parts. If it looks like a template, rework it.
- **Confidence without noise.** Approachable expertise. Clarity and craft beat decorative effects; motion and visuals must earn their place.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Target WCAG 2.1 AA: verified color contrast (body text ≥4.5:1, large text ≥3:1) against the dark surfaces, semantic HTML, visible focus states, and keyboard operability. The site is animation-heavy (GSAP, Framer Motion, SVG draw-on sequences), so every animation must have a `prefers-reduced-motion: reduce` alternative (crossfade or instant), and content must never be gated behind an animation that could fail to fire.
